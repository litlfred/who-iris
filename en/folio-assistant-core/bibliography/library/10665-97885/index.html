  29. S-12 (Sassani et al., 1991) 3.2 Immune responses to revaccination Studies have examined presence of antibodies prior to and following the second dose of mumps vaccine. In a prospective study, <1% of subjects were seronegative before a second dose of mumps vaccine and, following the second dose, IgM was detectable in only 2% of individuals, suggesting that most vaccine recipients had mounted a secondary immune response to revaccination (LeBaron et al., 2009). Although up to 30% of individuals were reported to be seronegative prior to revaccination in other studies, 75% to 97% seroconverted following the second dose of mumps vaccine.
