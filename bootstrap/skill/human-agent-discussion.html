descriptionAsk a person (or a sibling agent) for what no file holds. First determine where you are — the context, the Role you act as and theirs, the Process and task (or that you are idle) — and ask about whichever you cannot determine before anything else. Then context, the options, a recommendation and what happens with no answer, then ONE question.
