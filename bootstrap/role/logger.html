labelLogger
descriptionA machine that accepts log messages and puts them somewhere a person can read. It receives and records, and takes no part in what happens next. Its skill is its contract, `logger`: record a complete message unchanged and attributed to its actor. Composing the message is `log-message`, the skill of whoever is doing the thing being logged.
