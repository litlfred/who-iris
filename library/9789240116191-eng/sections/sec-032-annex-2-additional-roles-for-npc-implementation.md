## Annex 2. Additional roles for NPC implementation

The following table illustrates the typical technical roles that should be considered for NPC implementation. Resource matrix for DHSC implementation that is included in the digital transformation handbook for supply chain architecture can be referred for additional roles and details.

| ROLE CATEGORY | ROLES | PHASE(S) IN WHICH IT IS REQUIRED: DEPLOYMENT | PHASE(S) IN WHICH IT IS REQUIRED: OPERATION | POSITION TYPE | SKILL SET | RESPONSIBILITIES |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Leadership | Project manager | Y | N | Contracted/temporary | • Understanding of business requirements and high-level IT architectures/solutions<br>• Project management skills such as planning, budgeting, risk management, team management and time management<br>• Experience in various project management methodologies such as agile, waterfall and software development lifecycle<br>• Ability to coordinate with and manage multiple stakeholders<br>• Interpersonal skills such as communication, presentation and team development | • Set project objectives and charter<br>• Develop project plan to manage resources and timeline within the allotted budget<br>• Provide regular status updates to team and senior leadership<br>• Identify and assess risks and develop mitigation strategies<br>• Coordinate efforts of all team members to meet project objectives<br>• Monitor and manage project performance and progress |
| Leadership | Solution architect | Y | N | Contracted/temporary | • Knowledge of various technology options and trends, such as cloud services, software as a service and open source<br>• Experience in software design and architecture<br>• Knowledge of IT infrastructure<br>• Technical leadership skills such as providing technical guidance and vision<br>• Ability to understand business requirements and map them to technology solutions<br>• Understanding of best practices in software development, architecture, master data management and IT deployments<br>• Analytical and problem-solving skills | • Gather business requirements to define scope and develop technology solutions and architecture to satisfy requirements<br>• Prepare technology specifications to implement, using best-practices<br>• Analyse existing systems and identify improvements and integration requirements<br>• Guide technical team members in implementing technology solutions<br>• Coordinate with various technical teams such as integration specialists, infrastructure specialists and quality assurance (QA) personnel to guide them in various aspects of IT implementation<br>• Coordinate with senior leadership to communicate about technology options, cost impact and budget needs |
| Technical | Business analyst | Y | N | Contracted/temporary | • Analytical and problem-solving skills<br>• Writing and communication skills<br>• Ability to understand and gather business requirements<br>• Knowledge of supply chain processes and master data management<br>• Interpersonal skills such as coordination across various teams and stakeholders | • Gather and analyse business requirements and map business processes<br>• Collaborate with technical teams to translate business needs into technical requirements<br>• Develop use cases or user stories to convey business needs to technical teams<br>• Coordinate with QA team to ensure that test cases for validating technical solutions are developed according to business needs<br>• Collaborate with multiple technical and business teams<br>• Collaborate with QA and training teams to prepare test cases and training material |
| Technical | Developer/programmers | s | N | Contracted/temporary | • Knowledge of and experience with programming languages such as Java, Python and C++<br>• Knowledge of and experience with technology options such as relational databases, web frameworks and object-oriented development<br>• Ability to understand business requirements and develop programs to meet those requirements<br>• Interpersonal skills such as ability to coordinate various technical and process teams<br>• Analytical and problem-solving skills<br>• Experience in developing software solutions using open-source tools | • Design and develop software programs that meet business requirements<br>• Deploy the developed software across multiple environments, such as test, staging and production<br>• Troubleshoot and fix bugs in software programs<br>• Collaborate with QA and training teams to prepare test cases and training material |
| Technical | Integration specialist | Y | N | Contracted/temporary | • Knowledge of and experience with ETL (Extract, Transform and Load) tools<br>• Knowledge of and experience with SQL<br>• Knowledge of and experience with service-oriented architecture<br>• Knowledge of and experience with various data transfer protocols, schemas and formats<br>• Knowledge of available data transformation and interoperability tools and experience with at least two tools | • Design integration solutions to use interoperability features across multiple systems<br>• Develop integration standards and protocols<br>• Coordinate with implementation team members such as architects and programmers to develop application integrations<br>• Maintain the developed integrations and troubleshoot issues |
| Technical | Database administrator | Y | Y | Permanent | • Knowledge of various database tools<br>• Data modelling<br>• Database management, including performance monitoring, optimizing and tuning<br>• Database backup and recovery<br>• Data security | • Advise on relevant database tools to use<br>• Support database activities such as data modelling, performance monitoring, data cleansing and tuning<br>• Monitor databases to ensure timely backup and recovery when required<br>• Ensure security of databases and administer relevant access to appropriate users<br>• Coordinate with teams deploying various IT tools to support database activities |
| Technical | Infrastructure specialist | Y | Y | Permanent | • Proficiency with network protocols such as TCP/IP, DNS, DHCP and VPN<br>• Knowledge of firewalls, intrusion detection/prevention systems and VPNs for secure data transmission<br>• Proficiency with Linux, Unix and Windows Server environments for installation, configuration and maintenance<br>• Experience with virtualization platforms such as VMware, Hyper-V and KVM<br>• Familiarity with scripting (e.g., PowerShell, Bash) to automate system tasks<br>• Experience with cloud infrastructure services<br>• Ability to design, implement and manage cloud-based solutions<br>• Experience with SAN, NAS and other storage solutions<br>• Understanding of best practices in securing systems and networks, including patch management, malware protection and secure access controls<br>• Familiarity with regulations such as those ensuring that infrastructure complies with legal and regulatory requirements | • Define backup strategies and disaster recovery plans and ensure business continuity<br>• Optimize systems and networks for high availability and efficiency |
| Leadership | QA/test manager | Y | N | Contracted/temporary | • Knowledge of software implementation methodologies, including agile and waterfall.<br>• Knowledge of and experience with automated testing tools<br>• Experience with testing large-scale IT tools, managing test teams and troubleshooting test scenarios and technical issues<br>• Experience with testing phases such as system testing, performance testing, UAT and integration testing<br>• Experience with test case development, test results reporting and stakeholder management<br>• Experience with and knowledge of defect tracking tools such as JIRA | • Manage testing teams to perform test scenarios such as system testing and UAT<br>• Develop and guide the team in developing test cases and scripts based on business requirements<br>• Develop test management plans that include resource plan, risk management, timeline and scope<br>• Coordinate across multiple teams such as development team and infrastructure team to manage testing of IT tools at different intervals of the implementation<br>• Monitor testing progress and provide status reports to senior leadership |
| Technical | Test analyst | Y | N | Contracted/temporary | • Knowledge of software implementation methodologies, including agile and waterfall.<br>• Knowledge of and experience with various automated testing tools<br>• Experience with testing large-scale IT tools and troubleshooting test scenarios and technical issues<br>• Experience with testing phases such as system testing, performance testing, UAT and integration testing<br>• Experience with test case development and test scripting<br>• Experience with and knowledge of defect tracking tools such as JIRA | • Develop test cases and scripts based on business requirements<br>• Conduct testing across different phases such as system testing, performance testing and UAT and capture test results<br>• Coordinate across teams such as the development team and infrastructure team to manage testing of IT tools at different intervals of the implementation |
| Technical | System administrator | N | Y | Permanent | • Experience with IT tools’ system, network and database administration<br>• Knowledge of, experience with and certifications in various operating systems<br>• Experience with system administrative tools such as virtualization and VMware<br>• Experience with networking (WAN/LAN), hardware and scripting tools (e.g., Python and Perl)<br>• Experience with and knowledge of IT helpdesk activities such as data security and user management<br>• Troubleshooting skills | • Design integration solutions to use interoperability features across multiple systems<br>• Develop integration standards and protocols<br>• Coordinate with implementation team members such as architects and programmers to develop application integrations<br>• Maintain the developed integrations and troubleshoot issues |
| Technical | System administrator | N | Y | Permanent | • Experience with IT tools’ system, network and database administration<br>• Knowledge of, experience with and certifications in various operating systems<br>• Experience with system administrative tools such as virtualization and VMware<br>• Experience with networking (WAN/LAN), hardware and scripting tools (e.g., Python and Perl)<br>• Experience with and knowledge of IT helpdesk activities such as data security and user management<br>• Troubleshooting skills | • Monitor performance of systems and ensure uptime<br>• Manage various servers, networks and other technology tools to maintain operations of IT applications<br>• Monitor for technical issues and troubleshoot<br>• Ensure data security by adding/modifying/deleting user accounts and providing users with appropriate access to data<br>• Apply version updates, hot fixes and any other software/hardware revisions required for all the applicable tools |
| Technical | Support engineer | N | Y | Permanent or contracted/temporary | • Knowledge of and experience with supply chain software<br>• Knowledge of networking and hardware setup<br>• Experience with software support activities such as troubleshooting, coordinating with users and vendors, and applying fixes<br>• Proficiency in remote setup or virtual tools to troubleshoot technical issues<br>• Knowledge of defect tracking tools such as JIRA | • Provide day-to-day support to users of software tools to troubleshoot any issues<br>• Monitor performance of software tools and ensure uptime<br>• Provide regular maintenance of software tools and applying version updates, hot fixes, etc.<br>• Capture technical issues and resolutions for status reporting |
| Technical | Superuser/trainer | Y | Y | Permanent | • Knowledge of supply chain processes, master data management and features of relevant software tools such as PCMT<br>• Experience using two or more supply chain and/or master data management software tools<br>• Experience training end users | • Train end users on various functionalities and features in supply chain software tools<br>• Help users with system administrative activities that can be managed by end users (e.g., password change).<br>• Perform simple and basic configuration of systems, where applicable, to enhance or add features to software tools |
