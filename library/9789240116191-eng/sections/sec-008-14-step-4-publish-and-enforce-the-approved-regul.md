### Potential risks and mitigation

| POTENTIAL RISKS | MITIGATION |

| Manufacturers’ poor adherence to regulations. | • Assess the situation to gauge current capabilities, investment willingness, and the required effort and timelines for stakeholders such as manufacturers and service providers to meet regulatory compliance.<br>• Define a phased approach, in consultation with stakeholders such as manufacturers and distributors, if necessary, to facilitate realistic adoption of standards and compliance with policies.<br>• Offer stakeholders like manufacturers clear timelines to handle transitions and ensure compliance.<br>• Create resources like training manuals and webinars to help various stakeholders navigate the necessary steps for managing changes.<br>• Establish a global standards community network to share information, best practices, and foster collaboration. |
