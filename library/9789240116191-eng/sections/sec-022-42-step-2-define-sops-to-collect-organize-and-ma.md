## Step 2. Define SOPs to collect, organize and manage product master data

| INPUTS | STEPS | RESOURCES | OUTPUTS |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| • Standardized master data coding schemes and structures.<br>• Master data management procedures, including master data governance policies.<br>• Data sharing and data use policies.<br>• Change management plan for all systems and processes to adopt the NPC. | 1. Define SOPs to collect, organize, add, update master data, and manage data approval workflows within the NPC.<br>2. Review and validate the SOPs against the actual workflows in the NPC to ensure full alignment.<br>3. Facilitate the formal approval of SOPs to institutionalize their use by relevant users.<br>4. Define and institutionalize a continuous training and advocacy plan to equip users with the necessary skill sets to manage and use standardized product master data. The plan should include:<br>  - Training of new users and newly hired staff involved in master data management<br>  - Encouraging participation in global and open standards conferences<br>  - Conducting advocacy sessions for operational users, emphasizing the benefits of standardized product master data.<br>5. Train appropriate users based on the developed SOPs to ensure consistent and effective implementation. | • NPC SOP example | • Product master data management SOPs<br>• Continuous training plan |
