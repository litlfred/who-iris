The development of this handbook was conceptualized and spearheaded by the leadership of Alain Labrique, Director, Department of Digital Health and Innovation, WHO, Garrett Mehl, Unit Head, Digital Health Services, Department of Digital Health and Innovation, WHO, and Deusdedit Mubangizi, Director, Department of Health Product Policy and Standards, WHO.
