## Step 5. Define short- and long-term strategies to source standardized product master data

| INPUTS | STEPS | RESOURCES | OUTPUTS |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| • Endorsed master data policies and legislations (from chapter 1)<br>• Data sharing and data use policies<br>• Standardized master data coding schemes and structures | 1. Identify challenges with sourcing master data, such as incompleteness, data quality, data availability, awareness etc.<br>2. Define a short and medium-term strategy to source master data, while considering and addressing the identified challenges.<br>  - For example, gathering product master data from procurement agencies, donors, and physical packages when there is no efficient data sharing from manufacturers.<br>3. Define a long-term strategy to sustainably source master data.<br>  - For example, by formalizing product master data submission in predefined formats and data sharing mechanisms during the regulatory product registration process.<br>4. Develop a timeline to establish long-term data sourcing mechanisms and publish this as part of the regulatory policies to ensure awareness and adherence. | • Master data request letter - template | • Updated master data management procedures |

### Potential risks and mitigation

| POTENTIAL RISKS | MITIGATION |
| --- | --- |
| Limited awareness, momentum, and capacity among key stakeholders—such as manufacturers and distributors—to provide product master data in accordance with the policy guidelines. | • Conduct policy awareness webinars with manufacturers and distributors.<br>• Develop advocacy materials, such as one-pagers, that outline timelines, required data attributes, examples, and Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs).<br>• Adopt short-term strategies to source data while manufacturers and distributors prepare to comply—for example, collecting publicly available minimum data sets from product packaging.<br>• Begin with a smaller, essential set of product master data attributes, such as International Nonproprietary Name (INN), manufacturer, dosage form, strength, country of origin, and other critical information. |

### End of chapter checkpoint

After finishing the steps outlined in this chapter, the following outputs should be available:

- Master data management procedures including master data governance policies
- TOR for the master data governance group
- Standardized master data coding schemes and structures
- Data harmonization activity plan
- Data sharing and data use policies
- Short-term and long-term strategies for sourcing master data
