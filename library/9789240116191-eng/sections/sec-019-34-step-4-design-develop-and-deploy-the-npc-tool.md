## Step 4. Design, develop and deploy the NPC tool

| INPUTS | STEPS | RESOURCES | OUTPUTS |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| • NPC Implementation project plan.<br>• Approved functional and non-functional system requirements for NPC. | 1. Design the system and necessary configurations to fulfil all functional and non-functional requirements.<br>2. Review the design with relevant stakeholders and facilitate design approvals.<br>3. Develop the system and required configurations of the NPC tool and build integrations with other systems as required:<br>  - Ensure that the system accommodates the master data management procedures including coding scheme, data definitions and structures.<br>  - Ensure that the system comprehensively supports mapping of classifications, products and trade items and their packaging hierarchies.<br>4. Perform required validations of the configured NPC tool - such as system testing, integration testing, performance testing and UAT.<br>5. Ensure appropriate infrastructure setup such as hardware servers to host the NPC tool.<br>6. Deploy the validated NPC tool, load the available product master data and roll out. | • Data management considerations and best practices (Chapter 2) | • NPC system design and configuration specifications.<br>• NPC system validation report.<br>• Deployed NPC tool. |

### Potential risks and mitigation

| POTENTIAL RISKS | MITIGATION |
| --- | --- |
| Lack of technical capacity, resources, skill sets, and awareness of product master data management processes to implement the NPC. | • Identify and engage partners that can provide support—for example, funding organizations and implementation partners that can contribute technical and financial resources.<br>• Utilize the WHO-hosted PCMT as an initial system to begin managing product master data effectively. |

### End of chapter checkpoint

After finishing the steps outlined in this chapter, the following outputs should be available:

- Approved functional and non-functional system requirements for NPC
- NPC implementation plan
- NPC tool deployed
