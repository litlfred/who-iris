# 03. Deploying appropriate technology to manage product master data

Standardized product master data is intended to be used across various health and supply chain systems to enable uniform product identification. It is essential that this data is organized and managed consistently, with minimal manual intervention, to ensure data quality and integrity.

Adopting a suitable technology tool supports the ongoing management of product master data while maintaining consistent quality and compliance with defined standards. A widely used term for such a tool is the NPC, which many countries implement to oversee product master data.

This chapter outlines the key steps that countries can follow to select and implement an appropriate technology tool for establishing and maintaining an NPC.
