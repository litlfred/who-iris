## Step 1. Identify human resource capacity needs to support NPC

| INPUTS | STEPS | RESOURCES | OUTPUTS |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| • TOR for the Master Data Governance Group.<br>• Master data management procedures, including master data governance policies.<br>• SOPs for product master data management. | 1. Determine the human resource capacity required to manage product master data management processes, operations, and technical tools such as the NPC.<br>2. Identify each operational and technical role needed (e.g., Catalogue Manager, NPC System Administrator).<br>3. Define job descriptions for each identified role to ensure clarity of responsibilities and expectations.<br>4. Develop or update the existing human resource management plan, including:<br>  - Mitigation strategies for staff turnover<br>  - Cross-skilling and upskilling of personnel<br>  - Knowledge management practices<br>  - Identification of hiring needs to ensure sustainable capacity | • Catalogue manager role description<br>• Additional roles for consideration are provided in Annex | • List of roles necessary to support the NPC and product master data management processes.<br>• Job descriptions for each identified role.<br>• Human resource management plan incorporating NPC management requirements. |
