### Potential risks and mitigation

| POTENTIAL RISKS | MITIGATION |
| --- | --- |
| Preparing regulatory documents is often a lengthy process, and funding may not always be readily available. | • Identify and engage all relevant government stakeholders and senior leaders early on to foster a collaborative approach and minimise rework or repeated iterations.<br>• Identify and engage partners who can support with funding and other resources, such as technical experts.<br>• Involve private sector stakeholders, including manufacturers and distributors, to help ensure that regulatory documents are realistic and implementable. |
