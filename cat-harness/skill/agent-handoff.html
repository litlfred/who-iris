descriptionGoverns handing ONE task to an agent in another environment — a local agent with network the cloud session lacks, a sibling on a different machine, anything that cannot read the coordinator's context and that a person may relay to. States who writes which work item, where the receiver reports, what the person pastes (one sentence: work item, branch, repository) and the brief the work item opens with (second person, to the executor), what the receiver checks before acting, who closes, who may grant an exception, and how to recognise and answer the five ways an executor fails. Use when writing a work item for another agent to execute, and as the receiver before acting on one. Triggers on: hand off, handoff, local agent, agy, mac agent, needs network, executor failed, agent went silent, re-hand, "give me a branch and bean for them", relay, the other agent.
