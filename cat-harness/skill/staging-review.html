descriptionStaging preview and before/after comparison for content review. Takes one optional string — what the reader wants to look at, in their own words — and returns a list of URLs, each looked up in the publish ref, with what is published at it and what to review there. Provides before (MAIN) and after (branches/) pairs so reviewers can compare rather than guess. Used by the CRDM feature development workflow, content authoring review sessions, and whenever a staging preview deploys or someone asks to see it.
