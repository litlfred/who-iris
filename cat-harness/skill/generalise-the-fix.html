titleGeneralise the fix, then attack the generalisation
descriptionYou have a fix. Before it ships, ask what CLASS of defect it belongs to and whether the fix sits at the right layer — then attack your own answer: name what the fix now catches that the bug did not. Two axes go wrong, SCOPE and STATE, and widening either past the problem is how a fix becomes the next defect. Applies to a bug fix, a schema change, a detector, or a claim in a paper. Use after the fix works and before the pull request.
