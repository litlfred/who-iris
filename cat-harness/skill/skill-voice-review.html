descriptionReviews a skill against a voice that judges skills — the base `agent-skill-authoring` voice or a vendor override such as Claude's, Gemini CLI's or OpenAI's — rule by rule, with each rule's citation open, and records the verdicts with `voice:review`. Use when `skill-voice-review-current` reports a skill never reviewed or reviewed before the skill or the voice changed, or when asked whether a skill follows a vendor's authoring guidance. The agent half of that axis; the mechanical half, in `scripts/skill-voice-review.ts`, only says whether a current review exists.
