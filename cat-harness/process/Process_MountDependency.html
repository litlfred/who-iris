nameRemote-mount a dependency
summaryLaying a harness — and every layer it needs — down from another repository at a pinned commit, as declared directories, with a lock that says exactly what arrived.
descriptionLaying a harness — and every layer it needs — down from another repository at a pinned commit, as declared directories, with a lock that says exactly what arrived. You are in this process whenever a folio or harness needs a layer it does not hold: a downstream folio built on a content layer above this harness, a separated layer whose foundation lives elsewhere. The downstream names the harness, its repository and a 40-character pin as a `source.remote` layer in its root `index.config.json` (legacy: `remoteMounts` on its declaration, never both); everything else is read from the harness's own declaration at that pin. Every layer reached ends in one of three states — mounted, missing, could-not-determine — and the third is never reported as the first. The skill is `remote-mount`.
