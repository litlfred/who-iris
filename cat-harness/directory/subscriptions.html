titleSubscribed substrates
descriptionThe root declaration of each Knowledge Graph this layer subscribes to, cached at the pinned commit: one `<id>.substrate.json` per `subscriptions` entry, holding the upstream bytes, their sha256, and the harnesses and subgraphs the substrate offers. Written by `bun run cat kg:subscribe`; never edited by hand. Each CHOSEN part a subscriber materialises lands here too, under `<id>/subgraphs/<subgraph>/` or `<id>/assets/<path>/`: the upstream bytes under `tree/` and a `materialization.json` beside them with fixity, provenance and the five gates. A subgraph fetched in metadata mode (`kg:materialize --nodes`) lands under `<id>/nodes/<subgraph path>/`: its published `index.hydrated.jsonld` and a `nodes.json` record with its sha256. Written by `bun run cat kg:materialize`; never edited by hand. Nothing else writes here: an instantiated harness's state lives in `subscribed-harnesses/` (owner, 2026-10-30, bean `w1gy` finding 1, option C, "split the directory").
