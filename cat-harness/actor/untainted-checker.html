descriptionThe dispatched party that renders an artefact independently — a back-translation, an independent finding — given only what `UntaintedDispatch.checker_sees` allows and nothing that would let it shortcut. It RULES ON NOTHING: its witness carries `result: "n/a"` and the intermediate text a reader needs to ask 'on what basis?'. It deliberately does NOT hold `qa-reporting`, and that absence is the point — a checker that emits a verdict is comparing a thing with its own paraphrase of itself, and `check:qa-reviewer-permission` refuses it structurally rather than by convention.
