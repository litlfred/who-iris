descriptionDraws a board and performs what the reader has already decided: reads each kind's declared zoom threshold and the layout layer, swaps a card for its avatar, projects an open window onto the canvas, and carries out a confirmed relocation. A system lane — it runs a fixed program and exercises no judgement, which is exactly why every decision in board-open-close.bpmn and board-relocate.bpmn sits in the reader's lane instead. It is NOT the build pipeline: it runs where the reader is, once per request, and publishes nothing.
