descriptionValidates, renders and publishes, and watches whether that actually worked. A system lane: it runs a fixed program and exercises no judgement, so anything needing a decision belongs in a different lane. It carries `todo-manager` for the two CI-watcher dispatch points in `content-change-review`, which `claim` and `note` a bean and never `resolve` one -- claim is idempotent and note appends, both fixed programs, while deciding that a publish SUCCEEDED is the judgement this lane must not make (bean xom7: docs-site.yml failed 30 times while reporting its own exit code).
