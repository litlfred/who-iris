nameReview-log watch
summaryOne poll of one review log (a drafting Contributor Feedback sheet, or a public-comment sheet's Review Change Sets) and its folio repository.
descriptionOne poll of one review log (a drafting Contributor Feedback sheet, or a public-comment sheet's Review Change Sets) and its folio repository. A tool decides, with no agent judgement, whether anything a person set in the sheet or any open issue changed since the last poll. Only then does the agent append rows for new issues and do its pass. Skill: review-log-watch.
