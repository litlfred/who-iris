nameGuideline Updates
summaryTaking up a new version of a guideline, or a changed lower layer, is a re-pin and a review at once.
descriptionTaking up a new version of a guideline, or a changed lower layer, is a re-pin and a review at once. The steward maps the affected layers and re-pins them in dependency order. For each re-pin, the adaptation team compares the old version, the new version and its own adaptations. A change that touches no adaptation or policy merges on the technical path. One that does goes to the adaptation committee, which decides for each adaptation whether to keep, revise or retire it. Revisions are ordinary content changes (Process_ContentChangeReview), and the re-pin merges with them. The loop runs until every layer is current.
